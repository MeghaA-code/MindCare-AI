How the assessment works

Four short groups of questions feed a machine learning model trained to estimate a general well-being score — nothing more.

Multi-factor input

Personal, academic, social-media and lifestyle details are combined into a single picture of your day-to-day patterns.

Trained ML model

A model served by a FastAPI backend scores your answers in real time and returns a single well-being estimate.

Not a diagnosis

The score is a general screening indicator only — it never replaces advice from a qualified mental health professional.
